## User Summary

The `lwndev-sdlc` orchestrator currently lets the model walk past plan-approval and PR-review pause gates without the user's explicit approval (`workflow-state.sh advance` does not check the destination step's `context: "pause"` field), and it lets the model record a `record-findings ... auto-fixed` decision without any approval marker — together these allow the orchestrator to skip user-facing approval gates entirely. The fix (a) makes `cmd_advance` atomically auto-pause when stepping onto a `context: "pause"` step (stamping `pausedAt`, setting `pauseReason` derived from the step name, requiring `cmd_resume` before any subsequent advance), (b) removes `auto-fixed` from the `cmd_record_findings_review` decision whitelist entirely, and (c) adds an explicit `Output Style -> Load-bearing carve-outs` paragraph in the orchestrator SKILL.md stating that the "work without stopping for clarifying questions" system reminder does NOT apply to workflow-defined approval gates (`plan-approval`, `pr-review`, `findings-decision`, `review-findings`, `merge-approval`).
